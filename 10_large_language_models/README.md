# Chapter 10 — Large Language Models

> J&M *Speech and Language Processing*, Ch. 10 (Large Language Models).

How LLMs are built and used: the pretrain/fine-tune paradigm, how text is generated, what the context window is, and how performance scales.

- [`pretraining_vs_finetuning/`](pretraining_vs_finetuning/) — Fine-tune (or LoRA-tune) a small model and compare to the base model.
- [`decoding_and_sampling/`](decoding_and_sampling/) — Explore greedy, beam, temperature, top-k, and top-p decoding.
- [`context_window/`](context_window/) — Measure token budgets and the effect of truncation.
- [`scaling_laws/`](scaling_laws/) — Fit and interpret a simple scaling-law curve.
